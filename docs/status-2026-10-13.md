## How to look at it on Windows

Double-click **`Thread-Studio.cmd`**. It starts the local engine and opens Thread Studio in your browser: the 3D fitting room plus the asset-factory controls. Pick a kit, dress it, orbit or use the camera presets (front, ¾, side, back, face, torso, feet), switch poses (relaxed breathing, walk, arms out, knee bend), faces (52 expressions), lighting, wireframe and skeleton, and save screenshots. Enter measurements to build your body and add garments with flat-lay photos; jobs run on this PC and finished pieces appear automatically.

## Still not verified

- **Real photos.** The selfie test uses a synthetic render (grey brows, closed eyes) and the garment test uses a synthetic striped tee. Real selfies and real flat-lays have never been run; texture projection will need tuning on them.
- **On an iPhone.** Rendering of the kit on expo-gl (skinning, 52 morph targets, now about 50k triangles and a 2048 px skin texture), frame rate, memory and touch. The bundled kit grew by about 1.2 MB (base layer, larger skin texture).
- **TrueDepth scanning.** The Swift module compiles in CI; it has never run on a device.
- **Hairline.** Hair is a CC0 card mesh; from close up the front edge still looks cut out. The scalp under the hair now takes the hair colour, which hides gaps but not the edge.
- **Long sleeves and outerwear over tops** have not been inspected as closely as tees and trousers.

## Needs you

1. **NVIDIA GPU.** The RTX 5050 is still disabled (Windows error Code 43). Reboot or reinstall the NVIDIA driver; draping is about 10× slower on the CPU (a tee or trousers takes 2–4 minutes).
2. **Mac build.** Follow `docs/mac-iphone-setup.md` once; everything else runs from Windows.
3. **Your inputs,** through Thread Studio or the phone: height, chest, waist and hips; one sharp, front-facing selfie with open eyes in even light; flat-lay front (and back) photos of 2–3 garments on a plain surface.
