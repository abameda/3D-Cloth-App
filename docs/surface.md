# Native companion surface
Mode: Operate. User is dressing at home in mixed daylight; light surfaces make clothing color and photos easy to compare, with a deliberate dark adaptation for evening.

## Direction candidates
1. Dressing-room mirror: identity-led and familiar.
2. Stylist's contact sheet: visual comparison and editorial utility.
3. Outfit packing mat: pieces arranged spatially, individual slots held while the rest changes.
4. Music queue: a wardrobe sequence with held tracks and shuffle.
5. Tailor's swatch book: color families and touchable material inventory.
6. Museum collection drawer: searchable ownership and provenance.
7. Game equipment screen: visible slots and loadouts, restrained enough for daily use.

Assigned candidate 3, seed 38a982ca. User delegated detailed design; proceed code-led without another selection gate.
Challengers: cyclorama declined (lighting would distort garment color); keep explicit day/evening state. Tensegrity declined (force network obscures outfits); keep unequivocal lock feedback. Nixie declined (numbers are not the task); keep stable slot positions. Type specimen competitive in control clarity, weaker personal identification; keep immediate preview updates. Marketing monochrome declined (wrong task); keep one primary action. Busytown declined (dense labels hinder phone use); keep each garment identifiable.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An outfit packing mat that makes a complete look tangible, with native navigation and direct garment selection.
OWN-WORLD: Cool pale mint, forest action tint, ink text, rounded rectangular photo fields, system typography and light/dark semantic roles. No decorative gradients.
STORY: See the current outfit, hold the pieces that work, change the rest, save or record a real wear.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large Studio title; occasion control; spacious two-column outfit board; personal portrait/name strip; horizontal category rail with lock buttons; prominent Shuffle action. Missing garments are actionable slots, never fictitious clothes.
FORM: Outfit packing mat, candidate 3, seed 38a982ca. Signature interaction is holding individual slots while the rest of the outfit changes with haptic acknowledgement and a reduced-motion-safe fade.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
